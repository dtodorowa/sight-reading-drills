# Mission: Read piano music at a glance

## Why

I want to sit at a piano, see a note, and know what it is instantly, without
counting up from a landmark. Fluent reading is the thing standing between me and
just playing pieces I like. I want to close that gap fast, practicing in short
bursts on my phone whenever I have a minute.

## Success looks like

- I name any note on the treble or bass staff in under a second, from memory.
- I stop counting "every good boy does fine" and just _see_ the letter.
- I can move around the grand staff, including the tricky middle-C crossover and a
  few ledger lines, without slowing down.
- I practice most days and can watch my speed and accuracy climb over the weeks.

## Constraints

- Phone-first, offline-friendly, two-minute sessions have to count.
- Solo practice with tight, automatic feedback. No teacher in the loop.
- Weekend push to build the habit, then keep it going.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Rhythm reading and playing in time.
- Sight-reading full passages or both hands at once.
- Ear training and playing back what I hear (the mic idea is a later stage, tracked
  as GitHub issues, not part of the core reading drill).
